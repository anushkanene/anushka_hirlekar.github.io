About Me

Hi! I'm Anushka.

A product designer who spent 5+ years designing physical spaces before designing digital ones.

I began as an architect in Mumbai, then specialized in urban design — work that taught me to think in systems, design for how people actually behave, and balance competing constraints from stakeholders who all want different things. Those instincts turned out to be exactly what product design demands. Watching how people moved through built environments is what pulled me toward the digital equivalent: how interfaces shape behavior, and how thoughtful design makes technology feel natural.

After moving to the U.S., I completed UC Berkeley Extension's Professional Program in UX Design (2023–2025), building deep expertise in interaction design, visual design, user research, and front-end development (HTML/CSS).

My approach blends the analytical with the empathetic: asking the right questions, uncovering real user needs, and translating insights into designs that are purposeful and elegant.

I'm currently open to Product Designer roles — based in Pittsburgh, PA, and open to remote. Authorized to work in the US; no sponsorship required.

On a personal note, I'm happily married and a proud new mama — my baby boy arrived at the very start of 2026, and life has never felt more full. Becoming a parent has only deepened my belief in designing with empathy, for the moments that truly matter.
